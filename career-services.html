BEFORE YOU REQUEST CAREER SUPPORT

Prepare the information needed for your application

The more complete your information is, the easier it is to prepare accurate documents or guide you through an online application.

For CV & Cover Letter Services

Prepare your current CV if available, education and training details, relevant work or volunteer experience, key skills, achievements, the target job and the job advert or description where available.

For Online Job Applications

Have the vacancy link or application instructions, your contact details and the documents requested by the employer ready. You should keep control of your own email account, passwords and one-time verification codes.

For HELB Assistance

Keep the information and supporting documents requested by the official HELB application process available. Requirements, deadlines and procedures can change, so confirm current instructions on the official HELB platform.

HOW IT WORKS

A simple career-support process

1. Tell us what you need

Send the service you need and, where relevant, the job advert, application link or document requirements.

2. Share the necessary information

Provide accurate details and documents needed for the specific service. Avoid sending passwords, PINs or OTPs.

3. We prepare or guide

We work from the information supplied and help organize your documents or navigate the applicable digital process.

4. Review before submission

Check names, contacts, dates and supporting documents carefully before you submit an application to an employer or institution.

CAREER TIPS

Important things to remember

Your information matters: Only share information necessary for the service. PETECH SOLUTIONS will not ask you to disclose your email password, banking PIN or one-time verification code. Application accuracy matters: PETECH can assist with preparation and digital navigation, but the applicant remains responsible for confirming that submitted information is truthful and complete. Official requirements can change: Always check the current requirements, deadlines and instructions of the employer or institution concerned.
