CAREER

Build a CV that is easy to review

A useful CV should make your relevant experience, education and skills easy to find. Start with accurate contact details, a short professional profile and clearly separated sections for education, experience, skills and relevant achievements.

01

Tailor the CV

Read the job description and adjust your professional profile and skills to reflect the requirements you genuinely meet. Avoid copying phrases that do not describe your experience.

02

Use clear evidence

Instead of listing only duties, describe what you actually did. Where appropriate, include measurable results, responsibilities, tools used or communities served.

03

Check consistency

Check names, dates, phone numbers, email addresses, spelling and formatting before submitting. A consistent document is easier for a recruiter to review.

04

Keep sensitive data out

Only include information that is needed for the application. Never place passwords, PINs or one-time authentication codes in a CV.

JOB APPLICATIONS

Preparing for an online job application

Before starting an online application, gather the documents and information you are likely to need. A common preparation checklist includes an updated CV, relevant certificates, contact details, employment history and a professional email address.

01

Read first

Review the vacancy, eligibility requirements, deadline and application instructions before entering information.

02

Prepare files

Use clear filenames and check that required documents open correctly before uploading them.

03

Review entries

Check every field for spelling, dates and contact information before submitting.

04

Keep evidence

Where the platform provides a confirmation message or reference number, save it for your records.

ONLINE SERVICES

Using government and institutional portals safely

Online portals can request personal information, documents and payments. Use the official portal address and verify the organisation before entering sensitive information or making a payment.

01

Verify the website

Use the organisation's official website or a trusted route to reach its portal. Be cautious of links received through unsolicited messages.

02

Protect credentials

Do not share passwords, PINs, one-time passwords or authentication codes with a service provider or another person.

03

Confirm payments

Read the payment details carefully and keep official receipts or transaction references after a successful payment.

04

Check requirements

Requirements, fees and processing times can change. Confirm current information with the relevant institution before relying on an older guide.

DIGITAL DOCUMENTS

How to prepare documents for online submission

Good document preparation reduces avoidable upload problems. Use the format and size requested by the receiving platform, keep scans readable and make sure the correct document is attached to each field.

For scanned documents, check that all pages are visible, the text can be read and the file is not rotated incorrectly. If a portal asks for PDF, do not assume that a renamed image file is a PDF. Before submitting, open the final file yourself and confirm that it contains the intended information. Keep a private copy of important documents, but avoid storing sensitive files in public folders or sending them to people who do not need them.

SMALL BUSINESS

Simple ways a Kenyan small business can improve its digital presence

A professional digital presence does not need to start with a complex system. Begin with accurate business information, a clear service description, reliable contact channels and useful information that answers customer questions.

01

Describe what you do

Use plain language to explain your services, who they are for and how a customer can request them.

02

Make contact easy

Provide working phone, email and messaging options and check them regularly.

03

Publish useful information

Answer common customer questions through service pages, FAQs and practical guides instead of publishing only promotional messages.

04

Keep information current

Review opening information, service descriptions, links, contact details and important notices regularly.
