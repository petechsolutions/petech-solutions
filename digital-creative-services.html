PREPARE YOUR PROJECT

What to send before we start

A clear project brief helps us understand what you want to create and how the final work will be used.

For posters & graphics

Provide the exact wording, names, dates, phone numbers, logos, preferred images, social-media size or print dimensions, and any important branding requirements.

For websites

Prepare your business or organization information, logo, services, contact details, preferred pages, text and images. If you already have a domain or hosting account, keep the account information available without sharing passwords unnecessarily.

For digital documents

Send the source material and explain the required format, page size, file type, layout and deadline. Tell us if the final document must be optimized for printing, email or online submission.

PROJECT PROCESS

From brief to finished digital work

1. Share the brief

Explain the purpose, audience, platform and desired outcome of your project.

2. Provide content & assets

Send accurate text, logos, images and other materials that you have permission to use.

3. Design or build

We organize the content into a professional digital design, document or responsive website.

4. Review & finalize

Review the work for accuracy and request reasonable corrections before the final version is delivered.
