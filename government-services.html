PREPARE BEFORE YOU START

What you may need for an online government process

Requirements depend on the specific service. Having the correct information ready can make digital applications easier to complete.

Personal information

Have the identification and contact information required for the specific official service. Use your own details and check spelling, names and dates carefully.

Supporting documents

Keep clear, readable copies of documents requested by the relevant institution. Some processes may require specific formats, sizes or additional supporting evidence.

Your own account access

Where a service requires an account, use your own official account and keep your password, PIN and OTP private. PETECH does not need your one-time verification code.

SAFETY & OFFICIAL INFORMATION

Use official portals and protect your credentials

PETECH SOLUTIONS is an independent digital assistance provider, not a government agency and not an official representative of eCitizen, KRA, SHA, NTSA, HELB or another public institution. Official fees, requirements, approvals and processing times are controlled by the relevant institution.

Never share passwords, M-Pesa PINs, card PINs or one-time verification codes with another person. Before making a payment, confirm that you are using the correct official service and review the amount and purpose shown on the official platform.

For more online-safety guidance, visit the PETECH Knowledge Hub.
